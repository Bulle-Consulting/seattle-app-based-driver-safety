# OLS Research for Worker Rights Tab

## Three Key Ordinances

### 1. App-Based Worker Minimum Payment Ordinance (SMC 8.37)
- Effective: January 13, 2024
- Provides: minimum pay, transparency, flexibility rights
- 2026 Rates: $0.47/min, $0.80/mile, $5.34/offer minimum
- 2025 Rates: $0.45/min, $0.77/mile, $5.20/offer minimum
- 2024 Rates: $0.44/min, $0.74/mile, $5.00/offer minimum
- Network companies must report records quarterly
- Companies must be licensed + pay 10-cent fee per order

### 2. App-Based Worker Paid Sick and Safe Time Ordinance (SMC 8.39)
- Effective: May 1, 2023 (food delivery) / Jan 13, 2024 (all)
- Coverage: companies with 250+ workers worldwide
- Accrual: 1 day PSST per 30 days with at least one Seattle work stop
- Use: 24-hour increments for health, DV, school closures, safety
- Rate: average daily compensation over preceding 12 months
- Recalculated monthly
- Verification: only after 3+ consecutive days

### 3. App-Based Worker Deactivation Rights Ordinance (SMC 8.40)
- Effective: January 1, 2025
- Admin rules effective: June 24, 2025
- Coverage: companies with 250+ workers worldwide
- Key protections:
  - Certain deactivations unlawful
  - Must provide deactivation policies
  - Must follow procedural steps before deactivation
  - Must provide 14 days notice before deactivation
  - Workers can challenge deactivation
  - Must provide access to records
- Enforcement: Limited enforcement Jan 2025 - May 2027
  - OLS can investigate procedural compliance
  - Cannot investigate "permissible reason" until June 2027
- Workers can: challenge internally (within 90 days), then private lawsuit
- Ninth Circuit upheld this ordinance March 2026 vs Uber/Instacart
